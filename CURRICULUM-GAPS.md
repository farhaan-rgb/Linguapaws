## Specific calls needing a speaker

- **Telugu `panu` (L11) vs `pani` (L18)** — both glossed "work". పని transliterates as `pani`; `panu` looks wrong. Confirm and unify.
- **Odiya `tanka`** — glossed "His/Her" in L5 and "Money" in L27. These are different Odia words that romanise alike (ତାଙ୍କ vs ଟଙ୍କା). Needs distinct transliterations so learners and the answer-matcher can tell them apart.
- **Missing copula.** Hindi (`hoon`/`hain`/`hai`), Odiya (`achhanti`/`achhi`), Kannada (`iddeera`) are required in lesson 1 answers but never taught. Telugu is unaffected because it drops the copula. Add these to lesson 1 vocabulary.

## Transliteration consistency

- **Odiya copula is romanised three ways**: `achhanti` (L1), `achhi` (L3), `achi` (L8, L16, L18), plus `thila` (L20) for the past. All render the same verb ଅଛି. Pick one romanisation per form — the answer-matcher compares strings, so variants read as wrong answers.
- **Odiya `ku`** (the postposition "to") is required in L3, L11, L13, L14 and never taught. One vocabulary entry clears four answers.
