## How it actually went

**Lesson 20 was the good one.** It told me straight away it was revision — "A revision lesson, so most
of this is yours already" — and then tagged every word with where I'd met it: *Eeroju* "from lesson 12",
*Baga* and *Santhosham* "from lesson 16". That's the first time the course has bothered to do that and it
felt genuinely good, like the app remembered I'd been there. One new word (*Gurthundi*) and it said so:
"This one is new". Then it wired the new word into an old pattern — "it pairs with *naaku* the way *undi*
does" — which is exactly the kind of thing I want and almost never get. The sentence stage was short but
real: *Eeroju bagundi*, *Eeroju santhoshamga unnanu*, *Naaku gurthundi*, *Nenu ippudu paniki velthunnanu*,
*Sare, dhanyavaadaalu*. I could say all of those to a person tomorrow.

**Lesson 27 was the worst experience I've had in this course.** The vocabulary half was fine — money words,
easy. Then the sentence half asked me for three things it never taught me: "ask the price", "that costs one
lakh", and implicitly "I don't have". And when I said in plain English that I'd never been taught a word for
"price" or "how much", the tutor answered me **entirely in Telugu** — "ani adugandi." — two words I have
never seen, no translation, and it reads like a sentence that got cut in half. I told it I didn't understand
and asked again in English, and it said "That one has not come up in the course yet, so I will not guess at
it." So: the task requires a word, and the tutor's position is that the word doesn't exist in the course.
That's a dead end it built itself. I only got past it by guessing, and the guess was wrong, and it said
"Exactly!" anyway.

The other thing lesson 27 did was flatly overturn lesson 5. More on that in Q3.

**Lesson 30 answered my questions properly** — genuinely, four out of five, with real explanations. That was
a nice surprise after 27. But as a *finale* it's thin. See Q5.

**It is another five words with a thin coat of ceremony on top, and it does not end.**

What's good: `--status` calls it "Final Capstone". There are real callbacks — "I am very happy today" is

lesson 1/2 greeting-and-name material. The last two prompts, *"I am from Hyderabad and I grew up there"* and

itself. And it answers questions, which no other lesson I've played does.

What's not:
- **Of the five "new" words, two aren't.** *Santhoshamga* is already in my notes from L16 **and** L26.
  *"To say 'Telugu', say Telugu"* is a free step — the word is identical in English. So the real new

- **No callback to any actual scenario.** It never returns to the restaurant (L10), the shop, money (L27,
  the lesson immediately before), travel (L28), family (L17), the weather. It's five sentences about me.
- **It does not end.** The last thing that happens is `✨ Scenario complete.` and a five-word vocab card —
  **character for character the same ending as lesson 20 and lesson 27**. Nothing marks it as lesson 30 of
  30. No "you finished the course", no look back, no what-next. I asked whether I'd ever see the Telugu
  script and got "The course may show it later" — at the end of the last lesson.
- Sharpest irony: it teaches me **Aakhari, "last / final"**, and then never uses it to say goodbye.

I finished the course and didn't know I'd finished the course. That's the failure.
