# Telugu: questions for Farhaan

Yes/no questions on the items I'm least sure of, plus medium-confidence ones where the fix would change what the course teaches. Everything else is in `telugu-verdicts.json`.

1. **akkada vs akkadiki.** Is "Akkada vellandi" (అక్కడ వెళ్ళండి) wrong for "Go there", so it should be "Akkadiki vellandi" (అక్కడికి వెళ్ళండి)? (L3 p2/c1, L13 c0, drills t14-g3, t23-g1)
2. **Plural people with undi.** Should "Naaku chala snehithulu undi" (నాకు చాలా స్నేహితులు ఉంది) be rejected, so only "...unnaru" passes? (L8 c0)
3. **chedu.** Do people say "Adhi chedu" (అది చెడు) for "That is bad", or is it "Adhi baaledu" (అది బాలేదు)? Is "Chedu annam" (చెడు అన్నం) something anyone says? (L9 c3, t9-g2, t24-g2)
4. **Bare "hot".** Is "Bhojanam vedi" (భోజనం వేడి) acceptable for "The meal is hot", or must it be "Bhojanam vediga undi"? (t10-g5)
5. **naa vs maa.** Should family and home default to maa ("maa nanna", "maa illu") rather than naa ("naa nanna", "naa illu")? This would be a course-wide change.
6. **Having objects.** Is "Naaku pusthakam undi" (నాకు పుస్తకం ఉంది) fine for "I have a book", or should objects you hold be "Naa daggara pusthakam undi"? This is L5's core rule.
7. **melukonu.** Is "Melukonu" (మేలుకొను) everyday Telugu for "Wake up", or bookish next to "lev"/"lechi"? (L18 vocab, t18-g1)
8. **paduthundi.** Does "Vana paduthundi" (వాన పడుతుంది) mean "it is raining", or only "it rains / it will rain" next to "padutondi" (పడుతోంది)? (L28 vocab, t28-g4)
9. **Colour adjectives.** Is "Erupu batta" (ఎరుపు బట్ట) acceptable, or must a colour before a noun be "erra" / "tella" / "nalla"? (L19)
10. **oddu.** Should the romanisation be "Vaddu" to match వద్దు, with "oddu" still accepted? (L4 vocab)
