### 3. Lesson 30's last sentence drill — does the prompt match the Telugu?

**No — the order is inverted.** The English prompt is *"Say goodbye to Miko and thank her"*

### 10. Would I keep going? Did the course feel finished?

**Finished: yes, and that part is genuinely well done.** Lesson 30 knows it's the last lesson,
says so up front, doesn't re-introduce itself, spends the whole lesson recombining old material
on purpose, and closes with a specific recap plus a farewell in the two words I'd just learned.
I put the app down knowing I was done. That's more than most courses manage.

**Would I keep going if there were a lesson 31? Grudgingly, yes** — but the reason is momentum,
not the teaching. The grading is now honest: I tried thirteen different ways to bluff a step and
got nowhere, and the four legitimate shapes all worked, including the pronoun-optional case
which is explained better than I'd have hoped. That's the core loop, and it's sound.

What wears on you is being talked past. Eight of my eighteen questions were dropped, and the
pattern is perverse — asking while getting an answer *right* was the least likely way to be
heard. Three or four times the reply was the exact sentence I'd just read, re-served as if it
were an explanation. By lesson 22 I'd stopped expecting answers and was only asking because it
was my job.

The three things I'd fix first, in order:

1. **Answer bundled questions.** They're the ones a motivated learner asks.
2. **Stop printing the target sentence in reply to a probe** (attempts 16 and 23). The grader
   holds; the reply text gives it away.
3. **Label every word in a revision lesson.** `Sare` unlabelled, followed by the tutor
   insisting a lesson-4 word "has not come up in the course yet", is the kind of thing that
   makes you doubt everything else it tells you.

And the two-word teaching step in lesson 22 needs to actually require both words before it
counts them both as taught, because being quizzed on `Sahaayam` when I'd never once typed it
is the clearest "the course thinks I learned something I didn't" moment in the whole run.
