## How it felt

Three lessons, three different tutors. Lesson 1 was patient about pronunciation and generous with grammar insights, but sloppy at the seams — it ended on a truncated line, ignored my last three questions, and asked me for a word it had told me didn't exist. Lesson 20 was the best-behaved: labels with lesson numbers, cross-lesson answers that were actually correct, a clean recall phase. Lesson 30 knew it was the finale and behaved like one, right up to a send-off I'd screenshot — but it's also the lesson that handed me an untaught sentence when asked nicely, and told me a phrase was "in my notes already" when it wasn't.

The gate is trustworthy: I could not talk my way past a single step in twelve tries, across four different evasion shapes. The spelling checker is trustworthy too — four planted slips, four catches, no false fires, and it correctly refused to call a wrong verb ending a typo. Those are the two things I'd most expect to be broken, and they aren't.

What isn't trustworthy is word order (two clean reversals waved through, one of them contradicted by its own note in the same turn) and the question-answering layer. The pattern in the failures is consistent: when the tutor doesn't have an answer ready, it doesn't say so — it either reprints a teaching line as if it were a reply, prints a note from a different step, or tells me the answer was already above. All three feel like being brushed off, and the third feels like being blamed. Compare that to `This course hasn't covered a word for "and" yet.` — one flat sentence, and I trusted it completely. It already knows how to decline honestly; it just doesn't do it reliably.
